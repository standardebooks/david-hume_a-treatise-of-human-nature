# Production Notes

- Hume puts most proper nouns in italics or small caps, without any further semantic meaning than to indicate that these are proper nouns. Since the capitalization already indicates that they are proper nouns, I have not added this extra formatting.

- In the Selby-Bigge edition of the text, footnotes often occur just before their anchors (or before punctuation). These positions have been adjusted minimally to fit contemporary conventions (and, as with all SE books, converted to endnotes).

- In the "Appendix", Hume lists errata: paragraphs or notes to be inserted in the main text, as well as a few minor word changes that affect the meaning of the text. These corrections have been made in the Project Gutenberg text and hence in this production.
